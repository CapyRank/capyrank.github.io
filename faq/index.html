Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CapyRank.

General
CapyRank is a community-driven streamer discovery platform. Viewers give daily Capys to streamers they enjoy watching, and those Capys move streamers up a leaderboard within their follower league. It's a way for small streamers to get discovered based on community support rather than raw viewer counts.
Nope! CapyRank is completely free for both streamers and viewers.
Viewers don't need to sign up for anything. Just type !capy in any CapyRank-connected channel and you're good to go. The same goes for CapyGames: just chat. Streamers need to connect their Twitch account via the Join CapyRank flow, which takes about 30 seconds.
For Viewers
Type !capy in the chat of any CapyRank-connected streamer to give 1 Capy. Want to give more? Type !capy 10 (or any number) to give multiple at once, or !capy all to give everything you have left today.
Every viewer gets 100 Capys per day to spend however they like, across one channel or many. Your allowance resets at midnight UTC each day.

💛 Want more? Follow CapyRank on Twitch to unlock 150 Capys per day, an extra 50 to spread the love!

The home page ranks viewers, not just streamers:

🏆 All time: the most capys ever given, including capys won for streamers in CapyGames

🧭 Herd Explorer: how many different streamers you've supported. Find someone new and you climb!

🔥 Streak: how many days in a row you've given capys, in any CapyRank channel. Give at least one capy every day to keep it going; miss a day and it starts again.

Your own streak and explorer count show on your profile.
CapyGames & Yuzu 🍊
CapyGames are quick, optional games the CapyRank bot runs in chat while a streamer is live. Every so often the bot announces a game, you join with one command, and 60 seconds later the results are in. You earn Yuzu 🍊 just for playing, and winners earn bonus Capys for the streamer.

Streamers are always in control: games can be switched off at any time with !capygames off (see controls for streamers), so you might not see them in every channel. No sign-up needed, just chat.
Capys are for supporting streamers. You get 100 a day (150 if you follow CapyRank) and give them away with !capy.

Yuzu 🍊 are yours. You earn them by playing CapyGames and you keep them. Yuzu can never be bet or lost. No game ever takes yuzu away. There's nothing to spend them on just yet, but a yuzu shop is coming.
No commands needed: when a game starts, just type your answer in chat.

🏁 Capy Race · type a number 1-5 to back a capy. If yours wins, you earn yuzu and the streamer gets capys.

📦 Crate Drop · type 1-5 to pick a crate. One hides a capy (big win), one an angry goose (nothing), the rest a little yuzu.

🌊 Big Soak · type soak. It's a team game: if enough of chat soaks, the spring warms up and everyone wins.

🔢 Capy Count · type your guess 1-100 for how many capys are in the spring. Closest guess wins.

There are also quick-fire rounds, where the fastest correct answer wins:
🔤 Unjumble: unscramble the word
📋 Roll Call: count the yuzu in the row
🐥 Spot BeckyDuck: she's hiding among the capys, type her position

Everyone who joins a game earns at least 1 yuzu, win or lose.

🎃 In October the games dress up for Halloween: 🧹 Broomstick Race, 🎃 Pumpkin Patch, 🧪 CapyCauldron (type brew) and 🍬 CapyCandy, plus spooky words and a pumpkin patch for BeckyDuck to hide in. Same rules, same answers.
BeckyDuck is CapyRank's little yellow sidekick. She loves hiding among the capys, so keep an eye out for her in chat games and be the first to spot her! (Don't confuse her with the goose. The goose is grumpy and hides in crates.)

For Streamers
Click Join CapyRank, connect your Twitch account, then type /mod capyrank in your Twitch chat to give the bot the access it needs. That's it! Your profile page goes live immediately.
Twitch requires the bot to have moderator status in your channel in order to read your follower count, which is used to place you in the correct league. The bot won't moderate your chat or take any actions beyond tracking Capys.

They're on by default and share the same 12-messages-an-hour limit as CapyGames. Not your thing? Type !capyannounce off. This is separate from !capygames off, so you can keep one and not the other.
Yes! Raid another CapyRank streamer and you'll earn +25 Capys (once a day), and the bot welcomes your herd in their chat. Not sure who's live? Type !capyraid for a suggestion.
CapyGames are on by default. Not your thing? Type !capygames off and they'll stay off. You or any of your mods can control them from chat:

!capygames on / off: turn games on or off

!capygames 1-4: games per hour (default 2)

!capygames now: start a game right away (at most once every 5 minutes)

!capygames frenzy: start this stream's Frenzy

!capygames quiet: fewer announcements

!capygames status: show your current settings

Games only run while you're live and at least 3 people have chatted in the last 10 minutes. The bot never posts more than 12 game messages an hour, and your channel can earn up to 150 bonus Capys a day from games.
